# Surface brief: Marta Empire, variant 2 (staging branch)

Scope: the whole marketing site as a multi-page set: home `/`, `/services`, `/pillars`, `/appointment` (the VIP application), `/faq`, `/terms`. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: established female founders deciding whether to book the $666 Truth Session or apply for VIP Mentoring (3 × $4,997). Proof on hand: five real photographs of Marta, two factual prices, four anonymised client stories, facts from the Terms (Zoom, invoices, application is not a contract, 14-day withdrawal).

Pinned by the user (2026-09-24): a second variant, completely different from the black-and-gold site on `main`; white + burgundy with burgundy as the primary colour; sleek, modern, premium; Services, Pillars, Appointment and FAQ each on their own page; mood "modern minimal luxury"; a modern serif for headlines with a clean sans for body; the appointment page is the application form only. Carried-over preferences: little text per section, photographs used generously, every section animated with reversible scroll reveals, default cursor, readable type (no hairline Didone), graphics where there is no photograph.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A modern couture house on warm white paper. One burgundy, used like a jeweller uses a stone: the primary button, the emphasised italic word, the index numerals, the marks, and one full-bleed colour field per page. Architecture over ornament: a visible hairline column grid, square corners, 1px rules, photographs cut clean to the edge. It refuses the black-and-gold boutique of variant 1 (no frames, no seals, no glow, no ticker) and the pastel coaching template.

OWN-WORLD: Paper `#fbf9f7` with paper-2 `#f3eeea` bands; ink `#1c1517`; burgundy `#6b1428` primary, burgundy-deep `#4a0d1b` hover. Instrument Serif (roman and italic, 400) for display, index numerals and form fields; Geist for body, labels and controls. Headlines split into a roman first phrase and a burgundy italic last phrase. Ruled lists with small burgundy squares as bullets. Burgundy monoline marks (square-cut geometry, one 1.25 stroke) stand in where there is no photograph, one mark per idea. Added 2026-09-24 at the user's request for more graphics on the white ground: atelier "drafts", large construction drawings with dimension ticks and dashed guides (arch door, one column, compass, level), and printer's crop marks on section photographs. Buttons are rectangles: burgundy fill with a deep-burgundy wipe from below, or a 1px ink outline that fills ink. Text links are uppercase with a hairline that a burgundy line runs across.

STORY: Home routes (proposition, truths, two offers, four pillars, Marta, four stories, three questions, burgundy call to apply). Services sells (Truth Session first as the entry with Stripe checkout and what happens after; VIP Mentoring as the container with how it begins; for her / not for her). Pillars explains (four alternating rows, then the six decisions). Appointment converts (burgundy panel with who reads and the three steps; the seven-question form on paper). FAQ reassures (three topics, accordions, contact fallback).

FIRST VIEWPORT (home): Left seven columns on the grid: "Queen" roman and "Identity." burgundy italic at up to 10rem, a two-sentence lead, the burgundy button "Book the Truth Session" and the "Apply for VIP Mentoring" link, a ruled three-fact row. Right 40% of the viewport: the mirror portrait bleeding to the top, right and bottom edges, with a burgundy ME monogram square crossing its left edge.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The wipe. Photographs open downward (clip-path) while settling from 1.14 to 1; headline lines rise out of masks; a burgundy rule draws across the foot of each page hero; rules and marks draw on inside each revealed block. All scroll reveals replay in reverse when leaving the viewport. Continuous: Lenis inertia scroll, a slow scroll-driven drift on large photographs. The mobile menu is a burgundy sheet that wipes down. Reduced motion keeps state changes only.

DISCIPLINES: One burgundy colour field per page. One primary (burgundy) button per view. No eyebrow kickers above headings; the breadcrumb on inner pages is navigation. No cards with shadows; panels are ruled boxes. No coloured side borders thicker than 1px.

FINISH: this build ends with one finish review, one batch of fixes, and DESIGN.md regenerated from the shipped code.
