# Surface brief: home page (`app/page.tsx`)

Scope: the marketing site (home page, plus `/apply` holding the application form and the qualifier). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: established female founders deciding whether to book the $666 Truth Session or apply for VIP Mentoring. Proof on hand: five real photographs of Marta, two factual prices. Constraints pinned by the user (2026-09-23): the colour scheme stays black and champagne gold; medium length (about nine sections); serif display with sans body; rich motion; richer, layered compositions rather than plain spreads.

Rejected earlier the same day: an editorial refinement (too close to the old template) and a lookbook of eight spreads ("too plain and empty, layout basic, type and details weak").

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm boutique house on black velvet. Photographs in gold frames, a Didone voice, candle-light pools and ornament that feels engraved rather than decorated. It refuses both the glowing-card coaching template and the bare lookbook.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-900 ground with a still grain and a faint satin weave; bone type; gold as frame, numeral, rule, diamond and price. Bodoni Moda (optical size 96 for display, 48 for leads and numerals) for every heading, lead and pull line; Manrope for body and small uppercase captions. Ornament vocabulary: the rule-diamond-rule divider, gold corner marks, offset gold frames behind photographs, roman numerals, the MARTA EMPIRE seal with an ME monogram. One soft gold light per section. Rectangular gold buttons with a fill sweep; underlined uppercase text links. Offers sit in gold-bordered panels; everything else on hairlines.

STORY: She recognises herself (truths), sees the six decisions the work lands in, meets the four pillars and four client stories, feels the before/after, reads the two prices, meets Marta, has her objections answered, and leaves by the seal-crowned closing with two actions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left six columns: "Queen" then "Identity." in gold italic at up to 6rem, italic lead, one paragraph, gold button plus underlined link, a diamond-separated caption. Right six columns: the arched mirror portrait inside a drawn gold frame, a smaller framed bed portrait overlapping its lower-left corner, the rotating seal at its upper-right. Warm texture and two light pools behind.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The frame draws and the portrait unveils. On load the gold rectangle draws itself (stroke-dashoffset), the portrait rises out of a bottom clip while settling from 1.12 to 1, the second photograph and the seal fade up, and the headline lines rise from a mask. On scroll, every framed photograph unveils the same way and drifts gently (scroll-driven), section backgrounds parallax, caption lines rise in stagger. Continuous: inertia scrolling (Lenis), a gold ring cursor that opens over links with a slow pool of light behind it, the mantra ticker, the slow seal. Reduced motion and coarse pointers drop all of it except state changes.

FORM: Warm boutique elegance, chosen by the user from the structured question round after rejecting the lookbook (position 1 on the ordered list) and the assigned campaign direction (position 3). Seed key: ec86846e. Challengers declined; kept disciplines: one drawing-on technique used everywhere (the frame draw), identical modules repeated as the ruler (numerals and dividers), gold as material never wash, one gold accent per scene, one imperative per sheet, one emblematic image per section.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
