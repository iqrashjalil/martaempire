## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored, wearing a category costume. The hero (arched mirror portrait in a drawn frame, hallway shot over its corner, rotating seal), the engraved emblem system, the facts card over the desk portrait and the copy voice are this mentor's own. But every chapter opens identically (divider, display title, gold-italic last word, italic lead, seven times), the section order is the standard coaching landing page, and the mantra ticker is Instagram-coach idiom. Black velvet, champagne gold and a Garamond italic signal the category before they signal Marta.

Biggest missed opportunity: copy written and never rendered. lib/content.ts still holds the for-her / not-for-her list, the six decision bodies, the pillar "does" text and the Tier 01/02 labels; no component uses them. Partly deliberate (the user asked for less text), but the page's most specific proof and its most distinctive move (refusing the wrong client) are absent.

Deterministic scan: 16 advisory CLI findings, none blocking (12 font sizes, 2 colours, 2 radii). 12 are false positives: sizes and radii documented in DESIGN.md prose but not in its machine-read ramp. Genuinely undocumented: #e6c98b in app/icon.tsx:22 and app/apple-icon.tsx:20; 0.9rem in components/Invitation.tsx:71 and app/terms/page.tsx:34.

Visual overlays: injection succeeded only in headless Chrome; no user-visible overlay tab. In-page detector reported 34 anti-patterns, mostly the committed world (8 spotlight lights, 6 uppercase captions, marquee, satin stripes). Actionable: lead line-height exactly at the 1.3 floor; seal ring lettering overlaps its seam and is 54% occluded by the monogram; header transitions padding on scroll; featured panel pairs a 1px border with a 90px glow.

## Overall Impression

A handsome, coherent house with no clear front door. The visual world is finished and the form is lovely. Missing is decision support: which offer first, what she gets, what happens after paying, and why to trust this person with $15,000. Biggest opportunity: decide the ladder and let one gold action carry it.

## What's Working

- The hero composition: two photographs at different scales, one arched inside a drawn frame, the seal on the corner, the sharpest sentence beneath.
- The engraved emblem system: one glyph per decision, offer and moment, one stroke weight, drawing on.
- The application's pacing: one question at a time, hairline progress, keyboard accelerators, house-voice errors, signed success.
